# Open Board App
THe idea for this app is to have native iOS and Android applications that connect to my "open_board_led" board and users can create and illuminate boulder problems.

Here are features i want this app to implement:
  - [ ] Users take photo(s) of their wall, to upload to app.
  - [ ] Users can create new problems, which are saved to the app (locally or to google drive/dropbox, or to a cloud hosted database)
  - [ ] Users can tick/complete climbs
  - [ ] Users can create lists of problems
  - [ ] Filters by grades, stars, completed etc.
  - [ ] I want it to have feature parity to the "boardsesh" open source app but only support my custom board and not all commercial boards
